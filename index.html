Rob Surls

Founder, TexttASKAI • Systems Architect • Telecom Veteran

Operational Archeology was founded by Rob Surls after more than two decades in carrier-grade telecommunications engineering (Sprint, Ericsson). From early dial-up routing and SS7 signaling to SIP-T encapsulation and multi-vendor RAN integration, his focus has always been on what actually happens at the operational interface when systems collide.

Today, through TexttASKAI, he applies these battle-tested telecom physics to modern AI orchestration—decoupling transport from identity, eliminating point-to-point binding fragility, and building stable interface contracts for multi-agent systems.
